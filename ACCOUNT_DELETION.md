# How to Delete Your QuickBill Account

We value your privacy and provide a straightforward way to delete your account and all associated data.

## In-App Deletion (Recommended)

To delete your account directly from the app:

1. Open the **Settings** screen from the main dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section at the bottom.
3. Tap on **Delete Account**.
4. Read the warning carefully. **This action cannot be undone** and will permanently delete all your shop data, inventory, and sales records from our cloud servers.
5. Tap **DELETE MY ACCOUNT** to confirm.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For security reasons, if you haven't logged in recently, the app may ask you to sign out and sign back in before you can delete your account.

## Deletion via Support

If you are unable to access the app or prefer to have our team handle the deletion:

1. Send an email to **support@quickbill.com** (replace with actual support email if different).
2. Use the subject line: **Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include your registered email address or phone number.
4. Our team will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.

## Data Retention

Upon confirming account deletion:
- Your user profile and authentication credentials are removed from Firebase Auth.
- Your shop configuration, inventory, and transaction history are deleted from our Firestore database.
- Please note that any data exported locally (CSV backups or PDF reports) will remain on your device and must be deleted manually.
